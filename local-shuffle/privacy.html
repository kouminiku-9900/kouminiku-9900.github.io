Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Data we do not collect

Local Video Shuffle ("the App") does not collect, sell, or share any information, including personal data, identifiers, or usage analytics. The App does not request internet access and never sends data to the developer or any server. It contains no ads, analytics SDK, or tracking.

2. Access to videos on your device

The App reads video files only to play them in shuffle, and only within the scope you grant: all videos on the device (when you choose "Use device videos") or only the folders you pick ("Pick a folder"). The App never modifies, deletes, or uploads your videos.

3. Local storage and deletion

Display settings, selected folders, the list of videos you liked, and your position in the feed are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. Uninstall the App to remove this data. If Android backup is enabled, these settings may be backed up to your own Google account.

4. Sharing

Only when you tap the share button, the selected video is passed to the app you choose through Android's share sheet.
