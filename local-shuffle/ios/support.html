Support

Support page for Local Video Shuffle on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For bug reports, questions and requests, email kouminiku@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, please include the app version, iOS version, device model, what happened and the steps to reproduce it.

Common checks

  • If no videos appear, check in the Settings app that this app is allowed to access your photos.
  • If videos in a folder stop loading, select the folder again in the app.
