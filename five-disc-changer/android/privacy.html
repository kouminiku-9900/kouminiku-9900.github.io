5-DISC CHANGER PORTABLE · ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

How information is handled by the Android version of 5-Disc Changer Portable and this support website.

Developer: kouminiku-9900 · kouminiku@gmail.com

Overview

The app has no developer account registration, advertising SDK, developer-operated analytics SDK or advertising tracking. It does not transmit music files, library information, playback history, magazine names or disc names to developer-operated servers. The developer cannot remotely view this on-device information.

Information used on your device

With music and audio permission, the app uses Android MediaStore album, playlist and track identifiers, titles, artists, ordering, duration and artwork to load, display and play music. References and access permissions for files and folders selected in the file picker are also retained on the device.

Storage and purchases

Loaded discs, saved magazines and names, playback positions, modes and display preferences are stored on the device. Recovery copies may retain earlier configurations. Google Play Billing product identifiers, local prices, purchase tokens, signed purchase information and purchase, acknowledgement and revocation states are used to unlock, restore and update saved-set capacity. The developer does not receive payment card numbers or your Google Account password.

Android Auto and notifications

Song and artist names, disc information, artwork and playback positions may appear in Android media notifications, on the lock screen and on a connected vehicle display. Passengers or others able to see those screens may see this information. The app does not obtain location, vehicle identification numbers or driving history for this integration.

Google and file services

Product retrieval, purchases, restoration and purchase-state queries communicate with Google Play. Google handles its services under Google’s Privacy Policy. Play and operating-system diagnostics and backups also follow their respective settings and policies. The app has no developer-operated automatic log-upload feature. Selecting cloud-hosted audio through the file picker may communicate with your chosen file provider under that provider’s policies.

Support information

If you email us, we use your email address, optional name, message, attachments, app and OS versions, device model and reproduction steps to respond, investigate issues, help with purchases and handle privacy requests. Sending and receiving email services, including Google on the receiving side, process the message. The developer may process it in Japan and service providers in their countries of operation. We retain information as needed for the request and ongoing support, then delete unnecessary information. Legal or dispute-related records are kept only as required. We do not use support information for advertising or model training, sell it or share it for third-party advertising. Information may be provided to email processors or disclosed as legally required. Do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents or music files.

Website

This site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and connection information for delivery and security under its privacy statement. The developer adds no analytics scripts, ads or cookies. We do not track users across websites or change behavior in response to Do Not Track. We do not sell personal information or share it for behavioral advertising, regardless of Global Privacy Control signals.

Permissions, deletion and backups

Change music permission in Android Settings. File-picker playback does not require permission to the full music library. Deleting a magazine does not delete original audio. To remove local app data, use Clear Storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. OS backups or device migration may restore data; manage those through the relevant settings. Google manages purchase history and entitlements, which are not erased by deleting the app. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information or exercise applicable rights. We may verify identity and respond as required by law. The developer cannot remotely erase data held only on your device.

Children, security and changes

This is a general-audience music player, not directed to children under 13. We do not intend to collect personal information online from children under 13 without meeting applicable legal requirements. Parents who believe such information was supplied should contact us for appropriate action, including deletion. We use device and OS access controls and restrict support-data access; no method is completely secure. We update this page when practices change and provide notice or seek consent when required.
