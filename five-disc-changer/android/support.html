Support

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Help for the Android version of 5-Disc Changer Portable.

Developer: kouminiku-9900 · kouminiku@gmail.com

Contact

For a bug report, include the app and Android versions, device model and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords, payment details or music files.

Load music

Choose albums or playlists from the music on your device, or choose audio files or folders with the file picker. Library access requires music and audio permission; file-picker playback works without that permission.

Missing albums or READ ERROR

Check music permission in Android Settings. Music in cloud services or another app’s private storage may not appear in the device library. Check whether files were moved or deleted or access was revoked, then select the files or folder again. Download cloud files to your device before retrying.

Sources and formats

The app plays device music and audio files you select. It does not integrate with streaming services such as Apple Music or Spotify. Common formats such as MP3, AAC/M4A, WAV and FLAC depend on your device’s decoder and the file’s encoding. DRM-protected audio is not supported.

Restore purchases

Sign in to Google Play with the Google Account used for the purchase, then tap Restore Purchases in the app’s purchase screen. Check your connection. Extra slots are one-time purchases and are not lost when you delete a saved magazine. Contact support if restoration fails.

Refunds

See Google Play’s refund process. Eligibility and procedures follow Play policies and applicable law.

ALL shuffle

ALL shuffle is available when all loaded discs are distinct music-library albums. With files, playlists or duplicate albums, use shuffle within the current disc.

Saved data

Playing five discs and saving and switching two magazines are free. You can have up to 10 saved-magazine slots including the free slots. Replacing or deleting saved contents does not remove purchased slots. If purchase entitlements are revoked, for example after a refund, the save limit is recalculated. Existing magazines are not automatically deleted and can still be loaded and overwritten. You cannot add a new magazine while at or above the current limit. Saved sets contain references to audio. The app does not copy audio files or provide cross-device synchronization of saved sets or music. Manage original audio and backups through your OS or file services.

Android Auto and safety

Load discs on your phone before connecting. Disc selection, track lists and Now Playing are available on supported vehicle systems. Load music, edit magazines, change settings and make purchases only while safely parked. Do not operate the app while driving. Availability depends on Android, Android Auto and vehicle compatibility.
