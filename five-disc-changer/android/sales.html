What is sold

Playing five discs and saving and switching two magazines are free. You can have up to 10 saved-magazine slots including the free slots. Replacing or deleting saved contents does not remove purchased slots. Extra slots are one-time in-app purchases: add one slot or unlock the remaining slots to reach a total of 10. The all-slots option appears when it costs less than buying the remaining slots individually. Previously purchased slots are not discounted or refunded. Purchases add saved-set capacity; they do not sell music or playback features.

Currency and price depend on your Google Play country or region and other applicable conditions, rather than the display language, and may change. The app’s purchase buttons show current local prices. Check the final Google Play purchase screen before confirming. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. We charge no extra shipping or handling fee. You are responsible for connectivity charges.

Payment and delivery

Sales use Google Play in-app billing only. Payment methods and billing timing follow your Play settings and purchase terms. There is no sale or external checkout on this website. Slots are added after payment completes and the app verifies the entitlement. Pending purchases are applied after approval. Try Restore Purchases or contact support if a purchase is not reflected.

Requirements and restoration

A supported device with Android 8.0 or later is required. Product retrieval, purchases and restoration require access to Google Play, an internet connection and a Google Account. Restore entitlements using the same Google Account. Library access requires permission, while file-picker playback does not require permission to the full library. Saved sets contain references to audio. The app does not copy audio files or provide cross-device synchronization of saved sets or music.

Cancellation, refunds and issues

You can cancel in the Google Play purchase screen before confirmation. After purchase, see Google Play’s refund process. Eligibility and procedures follow Play policies and applicable law. If purchase entitlements are revoked, for example after a refund, the save limit is recalculated. Existing magazines are not automatically deleted and can still be loaded and overwritten. You cannot add a new magazine while at or above the current limit. Contact support for entitlement or app issues.
