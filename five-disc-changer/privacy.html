5-DISC CHANGER PORTABLE

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 29, 2026

kouminiku-9900 (the developer) provides this policy for 5-Disc Changer Portable (the app) and this support website.

1. Overview

The app has no account registration, advertising SDKs, developer-operated analytics SDKs, or advertising tracking. It does not send your music files, music library information, or playback history to developer-operated servers.

2. Information used on your device

  • With permission, the app accesses song and album identifiers, titles, artists, and artwork in your music library for playback and display.
  • It accesses folders, audio files, filenames, and artwork you select, and stores references to access them again.
  • Loaded discs, magazine names, disc labels, playback position, playback modes, and display preferences are stored locally.
  • Apple’s StoreKit provides product information and verified purchase entitlements to enable and restore additional magazine slots.

3. Storage and retention

Settings and magazines are stored on your device. The app may keep local recovery copies if saved data becomes damaged. It references original audio files instead of copying them into its own music library. Depending on your operating system and settings, app data may be included in backups or device migration.

4. External services

Playing undownloaded Apple Music tracks, retrieving files from iCloud Drive or another file provider, and loading products, purchasing, or restoring purchases may communicate with those services. Apple handles payment information; the developer does not receive your credit card details. Apple’s Privacy Policy and the relevant providers’ policies govern their services.

5. Support requests

If you email support, your email address, message, and optional attachments are used to respond and resolve the issue. They are processed by your chosen email provider and Google’s receiving email infrastructure. We retain this information only as needed for the request and ongoing support, and delete it when no longer needed. We do not use, sell, or share it for advertising. Disclosure may occur where required by law. You can request deletion using the contact below.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as your IP address for delivery and security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies to this website.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can change music library permission in iOS Settings. Files can be selected without music library permission. Deleting a magazine does not delete original music files. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local app data; Offload App keeps it. Manage backups through your operating system or service settings. Apple manages purchase history.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its revision date when practices change. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact kouminiku-9900 at kouminiku@gmail.com or visit Support.
