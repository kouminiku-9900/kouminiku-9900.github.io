Products and prices

The app, playback with five active discs, and saving and switching between two magazines are free. Optional one-time, non-consumable purchases add reusable saved-configuration slots, up to ten including the two free slots.

  • One additional magazine slot: adds one reusable saved-configuration slot. Up to eight additional slots are available.
  • Unlock all remaining slots: adds the remaining slots, bringing the total to ten. Offered when cheaper than buying the remaining slots individually. Previous purchases do not receive a credit or refund.

Deleting a saved configuration does not remove purchased capacity. Entitlements can be restored using the same Apple Account. The app does not sync saved configurations or audio files between devices.
