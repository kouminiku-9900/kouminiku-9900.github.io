5-DISC CHANGER PORTABLE

Your music. Five discs.

5-Disc Changer Portable is an iPhone music player that brings albums and audio files together in a five-disc changer.

Load your favorites

Play albums from your music library, folders, or selected audio files. Save a combination of discs as a magazine. Requires iOS 18 or later.

Build your collection

Save and switch between two magazines for free. Eight additional magazine slots are available as individual one-time in-app purchases or an all-magazines bundle, up to ten magazines in total. There is no subscription. The purchase screen shows the current price.
