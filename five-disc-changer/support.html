How do I load music?

Use the disc loading screen to select a music library album or playlist, a folder, or audio files from Files. Grant music library permission if you want to use albums. You can change permission in iOS Settings.

Missing albums or playback problems

Check music library permission and confirm that the track plays in the Music app. By default, the app selects downloaded music. To use undownloaded tracks, enable the corresponding option in the app’s library settings. A network connection and Apple Music entitlement may be required.

Which audio formats are supported?

When loading from Files, you can use common formats that iPhone can play, such as MP3, AAC (M4A), WAV and AIFF. Files with DRM and some other formats may not play.

READ ERROR

Check that the original files have not been moved or deleted, then select the folder or files again. For cloud files, download them in Files before retrying. Some audio formats may not play.

Restore purchases

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then restore purchases from Settings → Add Magazines in the app. Additional magazine slots are one-time purchases. Deleting a saved magazine does not remove its purchased capacity. If restoration fails, check your connection and contact support.

Refunds

Request a refund for an App Store purchase through Apple’s Report a Problem page. Apple determines eligibility.

Why is ALL shuffle unavailable?

ALL shuffle requires distinct albums from the music library. If you load playlists, files or duplicate albums, use shuffle within a disc.

Delete saved data

Deleting a magazine does not delete your original music. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local app data; Offload App keeps it. Manage backups through the operating system or the relevant service. The app has no account registration.

CarPlay and safe use

Load discs on your iPhone while safely parked, then use DISC SELECT, TRACKS and Now Playing on CarPlay. Read the driving safety, Apple Music conditions and liability information. Song information may be visible to passengers on the vehicle display.
