Support

A notification did not arrive

  • Turn on Daily roast notification in the App.
  • Check Settings › Notifications › Screen Time Roast.
  • If Screen Time access was denied, turn the feature on again in the App.
  • A Focus mode may delay notification presentation.

The estimate differs from Screen Time

The App cannot read the total shown in Settings directly. It conservatively estimates use in coarse steps such as “3h+”, so differences of tens of minutes and callback delays are expected.
