Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Data we do not collect

The App does not transmit, collect, sell, or share personal information, identifiers, or usage analytics with the developer or third parties. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking.

2. Screen Time and notifications

With permission, Family Controls and Device Activity process total device use locally in coarse steps such as “3h+”. The App does not retrieve selected apps, viewed content, or a detailed activity history. Notification copy is created and delivered locally.

3. Local storage and deletion

Notification status, delivery time, and the current day’s coarse estimate are stored locally in an area shared by the App and its extension. Delete the App to remove its related local data.

4. Third-party services

The App itself sends no data to third-party services. If you email support, your email provider and Google’s email infrastructure process your address and message.
