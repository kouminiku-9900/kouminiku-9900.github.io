Support

Published and updated: October 21, 2026

Add videos

Tap + and select videos from Photos or Files. You can copy a folder or link to the original folder.

Copy or link

Copied videos are stored in the app. Linked videos require access to the original file or connected drive. Add new files separately after importing a folder.

Cannot play a video

Supported files are MP4, MOV and M4V playable by iOS, without DRM. Check the source drive, file location and available storage. Videos in iCloud may need downloading first.

CarPlay

Video requires a compatible CarPlay system. The vehicle and iOS control video availability. Support on audio-only CarPlay systems is not guaranteed. Import videos and change iPhone settings while safely parked.

Skins and restoring purchases

Video playback and the White and Black themes are free. Honey, Cyber and Pink are separate one-time purchases that change folder artwork on iPhone and CarPlay, along with iPhone colors. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase, open Skins and choose Restore purchases. Check the price in the purchase screen.

Delete videos

Deleting removes app copies, thumbnails and library entries. Originals in Photos and linked source files are retained.

Contact

Email kouminiku@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version, device, steps and error message. For CarPlay issues, include the vehicle and head-unit version. Do not send video files, passwords or payment details. See the Privacy Policy for how support information is handled.
