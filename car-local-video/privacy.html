Privacy Policy

Published and updated: October 21, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy applies to Car Local Video Player and its support website. The operator is Alchemis Matsuda Engineering (developer: kouminiku-9900). Contact kouminiku@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Data on your device

Videos selected from Photos or Files, generated thumbnails, video and folder names, playback positions, and appearance settings are processed on your device. Copied videos are stored in the app container. Linked videos use access information for the files you select. The app does not upload videos or folder information to a developer-operated server. App data may be included in backups or device migration according to your operating-system settings.

Playback and connected devices

Playback uses a temporary HTTP source that listens only on your device. It does not publish videos to a developer server or across your LAN. When you use CarPlay or AirPlay, video, audio and metadata such as titles are sent to the connected vehicle or playback device. Other people, such as passengers, may see the connected display.

Apple and file providers

Product information, purchases, purchase history and restoration are handled through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment-card information. Fetching files from iCloud or another provider you choose may involve communication with that service. Providers’ processing, backups and operating-system diagnostic sharing are governed by their policies and your settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

The app has no advertising, developer accounts, developer-operated behavioral analytics SDK or custom automatic log-upload feature. Development Debug builds store a playback diagnostic log on the device. This custom logging is disabled in the standard Release configuration.

Deletion and backups

Deleting a video or folder removes the corresponding app copies, thumbnails and library entries. Originals in Photos and linked source files are retained. Use Delete App in iOS to remove the app’s local data; Offload App retains data. Manage backups and provider-side files through the relevant service. Apple retains purchase records used to restore purchases even after the app is deleted.

Support information

We use the email address, message and information you voluntarily provide to respond, investigate issues, assist with purchases and handle privacy requests. Email is processed through Google’s email infrastructure; see Google’s Privacy Policy. We retain information while needed for support and delete it when no longer needed, except for necessary legal or dispute-related retention. We do not use it for advertising or training data, or sell it. We may provide necessary information to the email-service provider or where required by law. Do not send video files, passwords or payment information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not add custom analytics scripts, advertising or cookies to this website. External sites follow their own providers’ policies.

Access, correction, deletion and changes

Contact kouminiku@gmail.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information we hold. We may verify your identity as necessary and handle requests under applicable law. We cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device. If our practices change, we update this policy and its date and provide any required notices or consent process.
